01Project

A prototype,
not a model.

Since 2020, PolyLoop has designed and built its hyperloop pod at Polytechnique Montréal. A multidisciplinary student team putting its ideas to the test on the track and in competition.

Different backgrounds make the team strong. From magnetism to mechanics, every discipline moves the project forward.

03Systems

Four systems,
one pod.

Propulsion, levitation, structure, control. Each field has its decisions, its documents and its open questions. Open one.

Propulsion+

The prototype described in 2025 runs on four 6-inch wheels driven by AmpFlow DC motors. Linear induction motors are being developed separately by the research team.

Double sided LIM · LIM Motors & PCB · Three-Phase Control

Read the research ↗
Levitation+

Magnetic levitation research explores supporting the pod above the track. The preliminary design and theoretical presentation are publicly available.

Levitation Research

Read the research ↗
Structure+

A carbon-fiber composite shell protects the pod’s systems. The team develops the internal structure, aerodynamics and test track.

Internal Structure · Shell · Test Track

Control+

Batteries, sensors, speed measurement and software connect the prototype’s systems. Data acquisition supports testing and control development.

06Research

Knowledge,
shared.

Our design decisions and research are documented. Five publications, from magnetic levitation to pod architecture.

Rolling today.
Levitating tomorrow.

The 2025 prototype uses four wheel motors. Alongside it, the team is studying rotating magnets to generate lift above an aluminium rail.

Simulations and early experimental work are documented. Integrating them into a complete pod remains a research objective.
